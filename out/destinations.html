Curated cultural experiences across the sea, woods, desert, and mountains
Our Destinations

Revolūtiō does not claim ownership over these cultures or places.

Rather Revolūtiō seeks to support locally led experiences and partnerships that benefit the people and communities of the region, and treat and honor the land with care, humility, and respect.

The Sea
Yucatán, Mexico
Primary focus for Year One.
The Sea represents creative renewal, cultural exchange, beachside residencies, coastal retreats, music, food, art nature, and meaningful travel across the Yucatán coast.
Potential offerings:
  • Artist residencies
  • Creative guest retreats
  • Beachside cultural experiences
  • Live music and chef experiences
  • Wellness, nature, and ecology experiences
  • Yucatán community partnerships
  • Private curated stays Events, pop-ups, and cultural gatherings
The Woods
Phelps, Land O’ Lakes, Watersmeet, and the Upper Peninsula
Primary focus for Year One.
The Woods represents northern lakes, forest, music, storytelling, indigenous partnerships, rural creative economy, historical memory, recording retreats, seasonal festivals, and regenerative community programming.
Potential offerings:
  • Recording retreats
  • Songwriter and artist residencies
  • Seasonal festivals and community events
  • Museum and history-based storytelling projects
  • Rural creative economy development
  • Outdoor art, music, and wellness experiences
  • Cultural exchange between Wisconsin, Michigan, tribal communities, and global partners
  • Retreats rooted in nature, story, and restoration
The Mountains
Guatemala
Introduced in Year One as part of the vision. Developed more fully in Year Two.
The Mountains represent creative elevation, indigenous culture, ecology, textile traditions, music, food, retreat experiences, and cross-cultural exchange.
Potential offerings:
  • Artist residencies
  • Cultural travel experiences
  • Textile, food, art, and music exchanges
  • Retreats and creative immersions
  • Partnerships with local artists, guides, cooperatives, and cultural workers
  • Future exchange between Guatemala, Yucatán, Wisconsin, and Arizona
The Desert
Northern Arizona, Navajo and Hopi Land
Introduced in Year One as part of the vision. Developed more fully in Year Two.
The Desert represents ancestral land, vastness, silence, story, entrepreneurship, cultural respect, local economic development, and future community-rooted creative initiatives.
Potential offerings:
  • Cultural learning experiences
  • Local entrepreneurship support
  • Creative economy consulting
  • Storytelling projects
  • Artist exchanges
  • Community-rooted travel experiences
  • Partnerships with local tribal members, artists, guides, and business owners
