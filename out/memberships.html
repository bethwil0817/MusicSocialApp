Global Culture Club Membership
A global culture club for meaningful travel, art, and connection.
Who is it for?
The Revolūtiō Global Culture Club is a membership for people who want access to more meaningful experiences, local connections, artist-led events, private performances, curated travel, and cultural opportunities.
What is Included?
Members can receive help planning experiences, booking entertainment, discovering artists, joining retreats, accessing private events, and creating custom travel itineraries across Revolūtiō destinations.
Membership Pricing
Your access to ongoing support and relationships across our network

Explorer Member

Best for people who want to stay connected and receive early access.
Includes:
  • Newsletter and member updates
  • Early event announcements
  • Access to member-only opportunities
  • Priority inquiry review
  • Featured artist updates

Culture Club Member

Best for people who want concierge-style support and priority access.
Includes:
  • One annual experience design session
  • Priority booking support
  • Early access to retreats and events
  • Member pricing opportunities when available
  • Artist showcase invitations
  • Help sourcing entertainment or experiences

Patron Member

Best for supporters, donors, frequent travelers, and people who want deeper access.
Includes:
  • Two annual experience design sessions
  • Priority concierge support
  • Private event invitations
  • Artist residency updates
  • Recognition as a Revolūtiō supporter if desired
  • Preferred access to VIP retreats and cultural experiences

Founding Circle Member

Best for patrons, sponsors, partners, and people helping launch the ecosystem.
Includes:
  • Custom concierge support
  • Private experience planning
  • Invitation to founding events
  • Recognition if desired
  • Opportunity to help sponsor artists, workshops, or community programming
  • First access to future destinations
